> Below are my personal reflections on my 12-week experience in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program, in the hope of helping the FCAJ team further improve the program for future interns.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Curriculum and Internship Roadmap**
The 12-week roadmap was well structured: the first 7 weeks covered foundational concepts (VPC, Compute, Storage, Security/IAM, Database, Data Analytics/NoSQL) before moving into proposing and implementing the final project. Thanks to this, by the time I started working on CloudBrief in Weeks 9-12, I had enough of a foundation to understand and deploy a multi-service architecture (EC2, CloudFront, S3, SQS, DynamoDB, Bedrock, CloudWatch...) without feeling overwhelmed.

**2. Mentor Support**
Mr. Nguyen Gia Hung (Head of Solution Architect) provided close guidance during the project implementation phase, always available to answer questions about architecture or AWS cost whenever the team ran into difficulties. His approach of letting the team propose its own architecture, deploy it, and troubleshoot issues on their own — rather than being told exactly what to do — helped me learn far more than simply following a set of instructions.

**3. Teamwork via GitHub Issues**
Splitting the ITSoldier team's work into 5 clear GitHub Issues (core, deploy, QA, frontend, data pipeline) starting in Week 10 let every member know exactly their scope and completion criteria, avoiding overlapping work while collaborating remotely.

**4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities**
Beyond AWS knowledge, I also learned how to write technical documentation (worklogs, the Proposal, the Workshop guide), how to collaborate using Git/GitHub workflows, and how to re-verify a deployed system against a clear Definition of Done — a skill I expect to use often in my future work as a Cloud Engineer.

**5. Community Day / Workshop Sessions**
The 3 events held at the AWS Office (the Workshop & Community Day, the Community Day, and the session on Enterprise Cloud Architectures) were great opportunities to meet the mentor, other intern teams, and broaden my perspective beyond my own project.

### Additional Questions

- What I found **most satisfying**: getting to personally deploy a real AWS system from start to finish (not just following sample labs), and seeing my own work running in a production environment.
- If recommending to a friend: **yes**, because the program gives interns the chance to build a real AWS project on a real account, rather than staying at the level of theory or simulated labs.

### Suggestions & Expectations

- I would like the opportunity to keep exploring AWS further, especially the operations side (observability, cost optimization), after this internship program ends.
